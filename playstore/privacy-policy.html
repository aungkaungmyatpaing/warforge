Warforge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Warforge is a single-player game. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server belonging to us. Your progress is stored on your own device and nowhere else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting and no user accounts. We never receive your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your files or anything you do in the game.

What is stored on your device

  • Which vehicles you have finished, and how many stars each earned
  • Your coin balance
  • Your sound, music and assist-mode settings
  • A copy of the update manifest described below, so the app need not re-fetch it

All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app. None of it ever leaves your phone.

Advertising

The game shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us, and it collects data from your device in order to select and measure ads:

WhatWhy
Advertising IDTo choose ads and limit how often you see the same one
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressTo choose ads sold in your country
Ad interactions — impressions and tapsTo measure and bill for the ads

We never see any of this. It is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the AdMob policies.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device. Where consent is required by law, the app asks for it before requesting personalised ads, using Google's own consent form.

Update checks

When the game starts it may request a small file called version.json from GitHub, to find out whether a newer version has been published. The request carries only what any web request carries — your IP address and the app's version — and no identifier of any kind. Nothing about you or about your progress is sent.

Children

Warforge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, write to us and we will look into it, although as described above the app collects nothing to begin with.

Your choices

  • Play offline. The whole game works with no network connection. Without one there are no ads and no update check.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android settings at any time.
  • Uninstall to delete everything the app has stored.

Changes to this policy

If it changes, the new version is published here with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
