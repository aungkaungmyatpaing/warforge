## Full description — 4000 characters max

```
Take a century of military engineering apart, and put it back together.

Warforge gives you twenty-one real vehicles, from the Mark IV that crawled across the Somme in 1917 to the nuclear carrier USS Nimitz. You assemble each one piece by piece — engine first, then the running gear, the crew, the ammunition, the armour — and then you open it up and look inside.

▸ ASSEMBLE IT
Drag each part onto the blueprint in the order it was really fitted. Six stages per vehicle, from the powerpack to the last fitting bolted to the turret. Parts from other vehicles are mixed into the tray, so you have to know what you are looking at — a Panzer IV driveshaft and a Spitfire tailplane are not interchangeable, whatever the silhouette suggests.

▸ SEE INSIDE IT
Every vehicle has a full X-ray layer. The engine, the gearbox, every crew position, the ammunition, the fuel, the armour — all colour-coded, all where they really were. Slide the cutaway open and watch a Panzer IV's driveshaft run the whole length of the hull. Then look at a T-34 with its gearbox at the back, and see exactly why it sits thirty centimetres lower on the same suspension travel.

That is the whole idea: not a list of statistics, but the reason one vehicle is shaped differently from another.

▸ READ WHY
Each vehicle comes with the argument behind its shape. Why the Mark IV is a rhomboid with its tracks running over the roof. Why the Spitfire's elliptical wing was worth the misery of building it. Why Yamato's eighteen-inch guns were already obsolete before she fired them in anger. Why an Abrams keeps its ammunition behind a blast door with the roof panels deliberately made the weakest part of the tank.

▸ TEST YOURSELF
A short quiz on each vehicle, written from what the cutaway actually shows you — not from a specification table.

▸ THE TWENTY-ONE

Ground — Mark IV · Renault FT · Vickers Medium Mk II · Panzer IV Ausf. H · T-34-85 · Centurion · M1A2 Abrams

Air — Sopwith Camel · Fokker Dr.I · Junkers Ju 52/3m · Supermarine Spitfire Mk V · B-17G Flying Fortress · MiG-15 · F-16C

Naval — HMS Dreadnought · SM U-9 · Admiral Graf Spee · Fletcher-class destroyer · Yamato · Los Angeles-class submarine · USS Nimitz

Every one is modelled in 3D at its real dimensions, in the camouflage and markings it actually wore — from the mud on a Mark IV to the white Balkenkreuz on a Panzer IV's side skirts.

▸ NO NONSENSE
No account. No sign-in. No internet needed to play — the whole game works offline. Nothing about you is collected or sent anywhere.
```

## Notes

- **App name** — the subtitle matters: "Warforge" alone says nothing, and Play search
  rewards a name that describes the thing.
- **Short description** is the line shown under the icon in search results. It is the one
  piece of text most people will ever read, so it names the three vehicle types and the
  one thing that makes the game different.
- Play strips most formatting from the full description. The `▸` markers and blank lines
  survive; asterisks and Markdown do not.
