Warforge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Warforge is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your progress — which vehicles you have built, your stars and your coins — is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Warforge does not collect, transmit or store any personal information about you. There is no analytics SDK and no crash reporting.

Advertising

Warforge shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob may collect and process data from your device, including an advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with the ads. This is done by Google, not by Warforge — we never see it.

  • Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob policies.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Update checks

When the app starts it may request a small file called version.json from GitHub, to see whether a newer version has been published. That request carries nothing but the ordinary information any web request carries (your IP address and the app's version). Nothing is sent about you or about your progress, and no identifier is included.

Children

Warforge is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data from your device.
  • Turning off your network connection stops both the advertising and the update check. The game is fully playable offline.
